A second beginning for every girl and woman left behind.

Behold Again International Foundation (BAIF) is a Ugandan non-profit working with vulnerable girls, teenage mothers, single mothers and economically disadvantaged women. We help them get back to school, stay safe, stay healthy and earn a living, so that they can raise their families with dignity.

Partner with us See our programmes

Community-rooted in Uganda. Constituted as a company limited by guarantee, without share capital.

Who we are

BAIF was founded by Ugandans and friends of Uganda who saw the same story repeat in rural settlements: a girl falls pregnant or is pushed out of school, poverty closes in, and the cycle passes to the next generation. Our name is our promise. We believe every girl and woman can be seen again, valued again and given the means to start again.

We work through holistic, community-based programmes that join protection and psychosocial care with education, health, vocational skills, small enterprise and climate-smart farming.

Our vision

A society where every unprivileged child, girl and woman lives with dignity, safety and sustainable opportunity.

Our mission

To empower children, girls and vulnerable women through holistic programmes in education, health, protection, livelihood development and psychosocial support.

What we do

Our fifteen constitutional objectives are delivered through six programme areas. Each one meets a girl or woman at a different point on her road back to self-reliance.

Education and re-integration

Getting girls and teenage mothers back into school, and keeping them there.

  • School re-entry support for teenage mothers
  • Fees, scholastic materials and mentorship
  • Family and community re-integration

Protection and psychosocial care

Safe spaces, shelter and counselling for girls facing violence, exploitation or abandonment.

  • Safe spaces and temporary shelter
  • Psychosocial support and referrals
  • Anti-drug abuse campaign for street girls and young mothers

Health, hygiene and SRHR

Information and services that let girls and women make safe choices about their bodies and families.

  • Sexual and reproductive health and rights awareness
  • Menstrual hygiene, sanitation and safe water
  • Maternal health linkages for young mothers

Vocational skills and enterprise

Hands-on training and starter equipment so women can produce, sell and grow.

  • Tailoring, catering, hairdressing, handloom and crafts
  • Welding, metal fabrication, carpentry and concrete works
  • Business, savings and group enterprise management

Agriculture and agro-processing

Livestock, crops and value addition for better nutrition, food security and household income.

  • Goats, pigs, poultry, rabbits and cattle rearing
  • Food and cash crop production
  • Processing and market linkages, including export

Environment and climate action

Helping communities protect natural resources and adapt to a changing climate.

  • Climate change adaptation and mitigation awareness
  • Natural resource management and tree planting
  • Climate-smart farming practices

Across everything we do: advocacy for the rights of children and women, community dialogue on participation and good governance, and leadership training that builds accountable, ethical women and girl leaders.

How we work with each girl and family

Our approach follows a girl from first contact to independence, working alongside her family, local leaders and government structures at sub-county and parish level.

  1. Identify

    Reach vulnerable girls and women through local leaders, schools, health centres and community volunteers.

  2. Protect

    Provide safety, shelter where needed and psychosocial support, with referrals to police, probation and health services.

  3. Restore

    Return girls to school or into a training pathway, and support their health and that of their children.

  4. Equip

    Build vocational, business and farming skills, and provide starter kits, livestock or tools.

  5. Sustain

    Organise women into savings and enterprise groups linked to markets and to Government programmes.

Legal form

A company limited by guarantee and not having share capital under the Companies Act, Cap. 106, incorporated through the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB). Not-for-profit, with no distribution to members.

NGO regulation

Operates under the Non-Governmental Organisations Act, 2016 (Cap. 109), and its 2017 Regulations, including registration with the National Bureau for NGOs and District NGO Monitoring Committees.

Local coordination

Works through District and Sub-county NGO Monitoring Committees, signs MoUs with host local governments, and shares annual work plans and reports with them.

Beneficiaries and communities
  • Founder Members Board is the supreme authority, guiding policy, asset disposal and major agreements.
  • General Assembly meets annually to approve budgets, audited accounts and plans, and to appoint or remove the Executive.
  • Executive Committee of seven members serves a five-year term, renewable for up to two further terms.
  • Three bank signatories are required: the Board Chairperson, the Executive Director and the Treasurer.
  • Annual external audit by qualified auditors approved by the Board and General Assembly.
  • Standing committees for community projects, children and women's rights, human resources, finance and administration, and procurement, plus a disciplinary committee.
  • Restricted use of funds: money is used only for BAIF's objectives and the purpose for which it was raised, and all transactions pass through the bank.
